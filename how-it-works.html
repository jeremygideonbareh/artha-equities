What to upload

One statement. Handled with care.

Your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) lists everything you hold across depositories in a single PDF. It is the one document we need.

  • PDF only, up to 25 MB. The limit is shown before you upload.
  • Password protected? Enter it on the upload page. It is used once, in memory, and never stored.
  • Other statements such as broker or demat holdings may be accepted. The upload page lists which.
  • Wrong file? We tell you plainly what went wrong, and you can try again.

Tracking

You always know where it is.

Your status page shows four stages. We email you each time one changes. If we need something from you, we say exactly what.

Estimated timings are set by Artha and shown on your status page.

01Received

We have your statement

Payment is confirmed and your file has passed its checks. Your order reference appears here and in your email.

02Generating

Your report is being prepared

The report engine reads your holdings and prepares a draft report and slide deck. Nothing is sent to you yet.

03In review

Your report is being reviewed by our team

A reviewer at Artha reads the draft. An automated check flags any wording that could read as advice, and the draft cannot be approved until it is resolved.

If something needs attention
Your order may show "We need something from you" or "We are looking into it", with a plain explanation.
04Delivered

Your report is ready

Download your PDF report and slide deck from your status page. Links expire for safety; you can request a fresh one at any time after confirming your email.

Your data

What happens to your statement.

A holdings statement contains your PAN, account details and full portfolio. We treat it as highly sensitive, from the moment it arrives until it is deleted.

01 · ON ARRIVAL

Encrypted on upload

Stored privately, encrypted at rest, never at a public address.

02 · IN USE

Used only for your report

Never for marketing, analytics or anything else. Your PAN never appears in logs or emails.

03 · IN REVIEW

Seen by few

Only named reviewers can open client files, and every access is logged.

04 · AFTERWARDS

Deleted on schedule

Removed automatically after a set period, or sooner if you ask.
