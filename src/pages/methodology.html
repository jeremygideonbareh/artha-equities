01

How findings are labelled

Not every statement in a report carries the same weight. So every finding is labelled by the kind of evidence behind it. You can see at a glance which points are read directly from your statement and which rest on calculation or wider context.

Observed
Derived
Context
Observed

Read directly from your statement. For example, the number of shares you hold in a company on the statement date.

Derived

Calculated from observed data using a stated method. For example, the share of your equity value held in one sector.

Context

Drawn from public market information to help you orient yourself. For example, how a sector weight compares with a broad-market reference. Context is never a forecast.

02

Data sources

Your report is built from two kinds of source. The first is your own holdings statement, which is the primary input for everything that describes your portfolio. The second is public reference data, such as company classifications and market-level figures, used only to add context.

  • Your Consolidated Account Statement, or another supported statement you upload
  • Public sector and company classifications
  • Published market reference data, cited with its date

Every chart and table names its source and the date it reflects.

03

What a report cannot do

A report describes your portfolio as it stood on the date of your statement. It cannot see holdings that are not on that statement, and it cannot know your goals, income, tax position or appetite for risk.

  • It does not tell you to buy, sell or hold any security
  • It gives no target prices and makes no forecasts
  • It does not promise or imply any outcome
  • It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional

04

How your data is handled

A holdings statement reveals your PAN, account details and complete portfolio. We treat every uploaded file as highly sensitive.

  • Files are encrypted on upload and stored privately, never at a public address
  • PDF passwords are used once, in memory, and never stored or logged
  • Your statement is used only to prepare your report
  • Only named reviewers can open client files; every access is logged
  • Uploaded statements are deleted automatically after a set period, or sooner on request
  • We collect only your name, email and mobile number at checkout

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, in how we give notice, ask for consent, limit use and delete data.
