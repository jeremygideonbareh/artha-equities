Who it is for, and who it is not.

A longer read, for people who want to understand the shape of their whole portfolio.

It is for you if

  • You want to understand how your holdings work together
  • You have read the Intelligence Report and want to go further
  • You value a careful, structured document over quick answers

It is not for you if

  • You want someone to tell you what to buy or sell
  • You are looking for forecasts or return expectations
  • You need personalised financial planning or tax advice

What it is not

Deeper does not mean advisory. The Strategy Report makes no buy, sell or hold calls, and no target prices.
