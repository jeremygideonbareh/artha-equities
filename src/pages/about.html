Artha Equities is a boutique Indian portfolio-intelligence firm. We bring the discipline of institutional research to people who hold shares and want to understand them.

Artha, अर्थ

In Sanskrit, artha carries the sense of meaning, purpose and means. We take the first of those seriously.

Most investors are told what to do. Very few are shown, clearly and patiently, what they already have. That is the work we do.

What guides us

01 / 06

Six principles.
No exceptions.

They shape every page, every email and every report we release.

01

Credibility first

Calm, precise and authoritative. No hype, no tips, no trading-app energy.

02

Non-advisory by design

We describe what you hold. We never tell you to buy, sell or hold anything.

03

A person in the loop

Automation prepares. A person at Artha reads and releases every report.

04

Your data is sensitive

Encrypted, used only for your report, deleted on schedule. No exceptions.

05

Evidence over opinion

Every finding is sourced, dated and labelled by the strength of its evidence.

06

Plain language

Short sentences. Jargon explained the first time. Nothing to decode.

Our approach

Careful work, quietly done.

Our benchmark is the printed research report: dense with content, generous with space, and honest about its limits.

01

Structured

The same clear sections in every report, so you always know where to look.

02

Evidence-based

Nothing asserted without a source. Nothing implied beyond what the data shows.

03

Reviewed

A person reads every draft before release, supported by an automated wording check.
