What you receive

Six sections.
Plain language.

Each section describes one aspect of what you hold, with its source and date. Delivered as a PDF report and a PPTX slide deck.

01 / 06

01

Allocation

How your equity value is split across market capitalisation and asset type, on the statement date.

02

Sector exposure

The share of your portfolio in each sector, shown as observation only. No view is expressed on any sector.

03

Concentration

How much of your value sits in your largest positions and in related holdings that move together.

04

Holdings-level observations

Short, factual notes on individual holdings, each labelled by the strength of its evidence.

05

Market context

Where your portfolio sits relative to the broad market, for orientation. Context, never a forecast.

06

Companion slide deck

The key findings as a PPTX deck, ready to read on a laptop or share with someone you trust.

Set expectations honestly

Who it is for, and who it is not.

We would rather you know before you order. If the report is not right for you, we will say so.

It is for you if

  • You hold listed shares and want a clear picture of what you own
  • You prefer evidence and plain language to opinions and tips
  • You can download your CAS from NSDL or CDSL as a PDF
  • You want a document to read carefully, at your own pace

It is not for you if

  • You are looking for stock tips or buy and sell calls
  • You want target prices, forecasts or return promises
  • You need personalised financial or tax advice
  • You do not hold listed equities today

What it is not

This report is a diagnostic. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security.
